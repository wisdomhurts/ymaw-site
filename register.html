The Young Men's Adventure Weekend is a supervised outdoor program for boys aged 12 to 17, run by a trained volunteer production team. Activities include hiking, paddling, bushcraft, fire craft, group challenges and camping, held outdoors in all weather.

I understand that outdoor activities carry inherent risks, including injury, and that YMAW manages these risks through trained leadership, safety briefings, appropriate equipment and supervision of every activity. I authorize the production team to obtain emergency medical care for my son if needed, and to contact the person named as his emergency contact.

For parents

Asked every year.

Is my son safe at YMAW?

Safety runs the weekend. Every activity is supervised by trained leaders, briefed before it starts, and matched to age and ability. The program has run for thirty years on that discipline.

Does he need outdoor experience?

No. Activities are structured so every boy can learn and win at his own pace, whatever his fitness or experience.

What does the $279 cover?

Everything: all meals, all activities, all equipment, and supervision from Friday evening to Sunday afternoon.

What if the cost is hard for our family right now?

Choose "Request financial assistance" when you register. We follow up personally and privately. No boy misses the weekend over money.

What should he bring?

A full packing list comes with your confirmation. In short: weather-appropriate clothing, a sleeping bag, personal items, and a willingness to try something new. Phones stay home.
